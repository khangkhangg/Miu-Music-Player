The song on your lock screen, and keyboard shortcuts.

- Lock the PC while music plays and the song shows on the lock screen with its cover, play/pause, next and previous. The same controls appear in the Windows Quick Settings and volume pop-up, and keyboard media keys and Bluetooth headphone buttons now control Miu.
- Keyboard shortcuts that work anywhere in Windows, even with another app in front: Ctrl+Alt+P play/pause, Ctrl+Alt+→ and ← next and previous, Ctrl+Alt+↑ like, Ctrl+Alt+↓ dislike and skip, Ctrl+Alt+M mini player, Ctrl+Alt+R bring Miu back, Ctrl+Alt+S sync. Change, turn off or reset them in Settings → Keyboard shortcuts.
