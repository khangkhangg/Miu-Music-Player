Playlists, DJ sets from a playlist, and a DJ that hypes the room.

- Playlists: make your own lists, drag songs onto them or use Add to playlist, reorder by dragging (or Alt+Up / Alt+Down), play, rename and delete.
- DJ mode can play from a playlist, in your order or in Smart order: it warms up, peaks about two-thirds in and comes down, with songs next to each other that mix well. When the playlist runs out it starts over.
- The new Auto style (now the default) hears what kind of music the playlist is (dance, hip-hop, pop, chill) and picks each transition to suit; ballads always get a gentle change.
- Hype: Auto DJ now performs inside songs, not only between them. Build-ups into drops (the filter opens, a riser, the last beats rolled shorter and shorter), a beat of silence before the drop, an impact as it lands, echoes on the end of sung lines and chopped breakdowns. Pick Chill, Party or Festival.
- The party screen pulses with the beat and counts the bars down to a drop: 4, 3, 2, 1, DROP!
- DJ mode opens on the Windows default speakers when your chosen speakers are not connected (and switches to them if they drop out mid-party), instead of refusing to open.
- An unplugged music drive no longer empties your library: its songs, likes and playlists are kept, the folder shows "Not connected", and everything is back when you plug it in.
