DJ mode and party mode: be the DJ, or let Miu run the party.

- DJ mode: two decks, a mixer, EQ, filters, echo, loops, hot cues and sound pads. Turn on Auto DJ and Miu mixes by itself: it reads each song's tempo, key, sections and drop, picks a next song that fits and changes over with one of 15 transitions (beat blend, drop swap, echo out, reverb wash, spin-back and more). Tick the transitions you like, or let it mix them up.
- Waveforms coloured by sound (blue bass, warm voices, white hi-hats) with the song's sections, hot cues and where the next mix lands, plus a zoomed view where both decks line up on the beat.
- DJ controllers work out of the box, and MIDI learn teaches Miu any other one.
- Party mode: put the party screen on the TV in its own window. Guests scan the QR code, add songs from your library or paste YouTube, Spotify or Apple Music links, and vote; the most votes play first. YouTube karaoke videos play on the party screen in YouTube's own player, nothing downloaded. Approve songs first, remove anything, set how many each guest may queue.
- Guests on your Wi-Fi connect straight to your PC; guests on mobile data can join through musicplayer.vietnamcos.com.
