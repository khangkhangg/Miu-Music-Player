Miu Music now updates itself. When a new version is out, a card at the top of the window says so, with what is new. Click "Update and restart" and Miu downloads it, checks its signature, installs it and opens again. Nothing happens until you click, so your music keeps playing. Settings → Updates turns the daily check off or checks now.

If you have 0.3.0, install this version by hand once (download MiuMusic_0.3.1_x64-setup.exe below). After that, updates come through the app.
