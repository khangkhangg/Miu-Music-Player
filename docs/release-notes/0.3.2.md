Lyrics, free. Hover the cover and press the lyrics button: the words appear in place of Up next. Synced lyrics follow the song, with the line being sung highlighted and kept in the middle; click any line to play from there. Miu uses lyrics already in your song files or a .lrc file next to the song, and otherwise looks them up on LRCLIB, a free and open lyrics database (turn that off in Settings). Lyrics found online are saved, so each song is looked up once.

Also fixed: the "Check for updates automatically" setting can now be turned off.
