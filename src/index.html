    YOUR IPHONE

    Before the first sync

    1. Open iTunes and click the iPhone button near the top left.
    2. In Summary, under Options, tick “Manually manage music and videos”, then click Apply.
    3. Keep iTunes open while Miu syncs. Songs already on the iPhone stay.

    Welcome to Miu Music

    1. Install Apple's drivers

      Free from the Microsoft Store (the Apple Devices app).

    2. Plug in your iPhone

      Use a USB cable. You can also skip this and just play music.

    3. Add your music folders

      Miu watches them and keeps up with new songs.

    Add your music

    Choose one or more folders to watch, or drop them here. Miu keeps up with new and removed songs.

    Songs
