    Before the first sync

    1. Open iTunes and click the iPhone button near the top left.
    2. In Summary, under Options, tick “Manually manage music and videos”, then click Apply.

              Connection

              Windows does not report Bluetooth signal strength, so Miu watches for dropouts over the last minute instead. Several dropouts usually mean the speaker is too far away or something is in the way.

              Second output (experimental)

              A second output plays only in shared mode. Turn exclusive mode off to use it.

              Put a microphone where you sit, halfway between the speakers. Miu plays a short test sound and sets the delay and balance so both arrive there together and equally loud.

              First plays laterSecond plays later

              Each Bluetooth speaker lags by 100–300 ms, speakers of different brands by different amounts, and Windows does not say how much. If the second output sounds late, slide left so the first one waits; if the first sounds late, slide right. Remembered for this pair of devices.

              A low click on the first output, a high click on the second, then both together. Adjust until the clicks are evenly spaced and the two in the third beat land as one. The music waits meanwhile.

              First quieterSecond quieter

              Speakers differ in loudness. Turn the louder one down until both sound even. Takes effect at once and is remembered for this pair.

              If both speakers can pair with each other (Bose Stereo or Party mode, JBL PartyBoost, Auracast), use that instead: speakers keep themselves in step far better than a PC can over Bluetooth.
